The platform

Everything between your prompt and production.

You keep your prompts and your product. Halo takes the failure modes.

Intent routing

Classify each request and send it to the cheapest model that has ever handled it well. Promote and demote automatically.

Semantic cache

Near-duplicate requests return in 12 ms. Cache keys are embeddings, not string hashes, so paraphrases hit too.

Budgets that bite

Hard cost ceilings per tenant, per route, per day. When the ceiling is reached, Halo degrades instead of billing.

Evals in the loop

Score every response against your own rubric. Regressions open a diff before they reach a customer.

Structured output

Ask for a schema, get the schema. Invalid generations are repaired or retried before your code ever sees them.

Replay

Every call is recorded with its context. Re-run last Tuesday's traffic against a new model before you switch.

How it works

Three lines to adopt. Zero to leave.

STEP 01

Point your SDK at Halo

Change the base URL. Your existing calls keep working, now with retries, timeouts and a trace for each one.

STEP 02

Name your tasks

Tag calls with what they are for. Halo learns which model wins on each task and shifts traffic there.

STEP 03

Set a budget and walk away

Cost, latency and quality floors are policy, not vigilance. Halo holds the line and tells you when it can't.
