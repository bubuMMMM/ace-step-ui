The programme

Eight weeks, each one built on the last.

Nothing is unlocked by a streak. If you miss four days the programme waits, and week three is still week three when you come back.

Week 01 – 02

Find the floor

Learn where your breath actually starts. Two exercises, five minutes, lying down. Most people are surprised.

Week 03 – 04

Slow the exhale

Extend the out-breath past the in-breath. This is the whole trick, and it takes a fortnight to feel ordinary.

Week 05 – 06

Carry it upright

Move the practice off the floor and into a chair, a queue, a difficult meeting. Ten minutes, split.

Week 07 – 08

Keep it without us

Build the version you will still do in March. Your coach writes it with you, then steps back.

The method

Boring, repeatable, and a little bit unfashionable.

What you get

Everything arrives by email at the hour you choose. Nothing needs an app open.

  • A ten minute audio each morning
  • One written check-in a week, answered by a person
  • A printable card for the two core exercises
  • A short sleep protocol from week three

What we won't do

Most of what makes wellness apps profitable also makes them exhausting.

  • No streaks, badges or leaderboards
  • No heart-rate data we can't act on
  • No upsell to a supplement
  • No claim that breathing cures anything

Questions

The ones people actually ask.

Is this therapy?

No. It is a breathing and sleep practice. If you are being treated for anxiety, panic or a respiratory condition, bring this to whoever is treating you first — several of the exercises are unsuitable during an acute episode.

What if I miss a week?

Nothing happens. The programme is paused, not failed. Your coach will send one note asking whether you want to restart the week or carry on, and that is the extent of the chasing.

Do I need anything?

A floor, headphones and eight weeks of ten minutes. No mat, no wearable, no subscription to anything else.

Who reads the check-ins?

Ilse, or one of two other physiotherapists during holidays. Never a model, and never a template with your first name pasted in.

Week one is free, and quiet.

Seven mornings, ten minutes each. If nothing has shifted by Sunday, that is a useful answer too.
