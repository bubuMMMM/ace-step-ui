The lookbook — colour studies, shot in one afternoon of flat light

We buy the cloth first and design backwards from it.

A mill in Biella weaves eleven metres more than we ordered, every time, because that is the smallest run their looms will accept. Those eleven metres decide the collection. If the cloth is stiff we cut a coat. If it drapes we cut a shirt and let it fall.

Nothing here is graded from a block that fits nobody. Each pattern is cut on a body, adjusted on the same body a week later, and then left alone. Seams are flat-felled where they take strain and left raw where they do not.

When the cloth runs out the piece is finished. There is no restock, no second colourway, and no version of this coat next season with a different button.
